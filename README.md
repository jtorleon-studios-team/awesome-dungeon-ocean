## Intro :

This mod adds a variety of structures (dungeon, temple, ruin, boat) to your ocean, enriching your exploration experience. The structures are designed with mob spawners and chests containing loot, providing resources and opportunities to interact with the environment.

- 🌊 Ocean Temple Quad
- 🌊 Ocean Quad
- 🌊 Ocean Warn Temple
- 🌊 Ocean Ruin Pillager
- 🌊 Ocean Ruin Vanilla Temple
- 🌊 Ocean Column Temple
- 🌊 Ocean Cage
